Flagship Hardware

MAESTRO
SWITCHOVER DEVICE

The MAESTRO Switchover Device is Urban Gaz's patented IoT solution for uninterrupted LPG gas supply. It continuously monitors the active manifold bank's pressure. The moment a pressure drop is detected, MAESTRO automatically switches the supply line to the reserve manifold bank — ensuring zero interruption to residents. Available with a coloured TFT display for instant status visibility, or a compact OLED display for space-constrained installations. Communicates via GSM for reliable city-wide connectivity.
